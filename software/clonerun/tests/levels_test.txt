# Test levels for the TEST build (CRT.COM) and test.sh, our own, in the
# form of Lode Runner's levels file (tools/levels.py): "=== NNN ===" and
# 16 rows of 28, . or space empty, # brick, @ solid, H ladder, - bar,
# X false brick, S hidden ladder, $ gold, 0 a guard, & the runner.
#
# 1: run into a brick at column 8 and a solid block at column 0.
# 2: up a ladder to its top, and down again.
# 3: off a ledge onto a bar and along it, dropping off it, a dig from it.
# 4: off a ledge one brick thick, falling to the floor, and through a hole.
# 5: digging in a brick floor on solid, the holes one deep.
# 6: digs refused: brick beside the runner, solid to dig.
# 7: digs refused: a ladder to dig, a ladder beside. A dig from a ladder.
# 8: through a false brick on the left and gold on the right.
# 9: a hidden ladder, not there in play: UP at its foot does nothing.
# 10: a guard on a solid floor, walking to the runner.
# 11: a guard on a brick floor, the runner to dig in front of it.
# 12: the same with the guard at the far end.
# 13: five guards about a ladder, the runner beside it.
# 14: a guard and gold, for the game's end and the lives.
# 15: a guard on the runner's row with a brick between them.
# 16: two guards in a line, a near ladder and a far one.
# 17: the runner on top of a ladder, for the keys.
# 18: a hole between a guard and the runner, a ladder behind the guard.
# 19: the last gold, then hidden ladders to row 0.
