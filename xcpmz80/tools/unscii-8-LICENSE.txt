License of the xcpmz80 console font

The console font, src/font.bin, is made from unscii-8 by
tools/mkfont.py. unscii-8 is in the public domain. src/font.bin is also
in the public domain. It is not covered by the MIT license in LICENSE,
which covers the rest of xcpmz80.

Font: unscii-8, version 2.1, the 8x8 variant of Unscii
Author: viznut (Ville-Matias Heikkila)
Home page: http://viznut.fi/unscii/ (also http://www.pelulamu.net/unscii/)
File used: http://viznut.fi/unscii/unscii-8.hex, kept here as
tools/unscii-8.hex, unmodified
SHA-256 of that file:
03094f7fbab7085cf6a6b624cee61e47e71ce5d0c2f308c2f4436afdc17f776c

The license, in the author's words, from unscii.txt in
unscii-2.1-src.tar.gz on the home page:

    Everything made by me (viznut) is in the Public Domain.

    The first source font, Fixedsys Excelsior, is also in the Public
    Domain.

    Those pieces that come from the Unifont project (unifont.hex,
    hex2bdf.pl) are under GPL. For this reason, unscii-16-full also
    falls under GPL.

And from the home page:

    "unscii-16-full" falls under GPL because of how Unifont is licensed;
    the other variants are in the Public Domain.

unscii-8 is not unscii-16-full and holds no Unifont glyphs, so it is in
the public domain. No GPL part of Unscii is used here.

What mkfont.py changes, also placed in the public domain: it takes the
ASCII glyphs 20h-7Eh as they are and U+2592 (medium shade) for 7Fh,
the cursor. It adds a second style for the MAX-80 font RAM, 20h-5Fh at
80h-BFh and 60h-7Fh at 00h-1Fh, drawn thin (the rightmost dot of each
run of dots cleared) or, with --inverse, inverted. src/font.bin is the
thin face with glyphs redrawn by hand, also public domain.
